Ambo Town Food Recovery Organization

The Ambo Town Food Recovery Organization operates as a municipal system fighting food insecurity and reducing waste. Our platform coordinates real-time recovery between commercial sources (hotels, restaurants, agricultural hubs) and local distribution sectors in Ambo Town.

Food Recovery Categories

1. Commercial Donors

Hotels, restaurants, and market vendors listing fresh food or cash contributions.

2. Recipient Agencies

Eligible care initiatives (≤ 5,000 Birr household criteria) receiving food supplies.

3. Logistics Network

Coordinated routes managing rapid food transport across municipal zones.

4. Municipal Oversight

Analytical monitoring directed by Mr. Hachalu tracking distribution and impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who manages municipal administration for this project?

Operations and compliance metrics are overseen under municipal leadership by Mr. Hachalu.

2. What are the income threshold rules?

Households with income less than or equal to 5,000 Birr/month qualify for food allocation. Donors with income over 5,000 Birr/month can contribute food or cash.

3. Who developed this platform?

Students from Ambo Ifa Boru Special Boarding Secondary School, trained at the Ambo University STEM Center.

4. Can donors contribute cash?

Yes, donors can select cash contribution in the portal to assist operational transport costs.
