Ambo Town Food Recovery Organization

The Ambo Town Food Recovery Organization operates as a pioneering municipal initiative dedicated to solving food insecurity and eliminating organic waste. Built around modern food recovery architecture, our platform orchestrates real-time logistical handoffs between commercial suppliers (hotels, restaurants, agricultural markets) and distribution hubs across Ambo Town.

Food Recovery Categories

1. Commercial Donors

Hotels, restaurants, and market vendors listing fresh, safe food surpluses for scheduled recovery.

2. Recipient Agencies

Community care initiatives and regional distribution sites receiving direct food allocations.

3. Logistics Network

Structured community channels ensuring rapid and safe food transportation across municipal sectors.

4. Municipal Tracking

Data analytics monitoring total rescued meals and localized community impact in Ambo Town.
