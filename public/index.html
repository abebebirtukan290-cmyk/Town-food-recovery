Ambo Town Food Organization Center

The Ambo Town Municipal Food Recovery Organization provides dedicated civic logistics connecting local food businesses, hotels, and agricultural donors with distribution networks. Managed under municipal leadership with administrative oversight from Mr. Hachalu, our service ensures that surplus food is safely redirected to those who need it most across all districts of Ambo Town.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who manages municipal food operations in Ambo Town?

Operations and district logistics are coordinated under municipal leadership with guidance from Mr. Hachalu and community coordinators.

2. How do local residents access services in their native language?

Use the language selector at the top right corner to switch between Afaan Oromoo, Amharic, English, Tigrinya, and Somali instantly.

3. Who developed this software platform?

This application was developed by full-stack developers from Ambo Ifa Boru Special Boarding Secondary School trained at the Ambo University STEM Center.

4. How is community input collected?

Inputs from leaders like Mr. Tena Feyisa, Ms. Marsimoy Shawul, and Mr. Dechasa Yadeta are stored using local persistent storage to ensure continuous feedback.

5. Is this food recovery service free?

Yes, this is a 100% free municipal service aimed at reducing food waste and supporting local welfare across Ambo Town.
