Income Gating Policy ($1,000 Rule)

  • Monthly Salary > $1,000 USD: Automatically classified as Donor. Recipient aid requests are blocked by server logic.
  • Monthly Salary ≤ $1,000 USD: Unlocks eligibility for Recipient (Acceptor) Status.
  • Urgent Need Queue: Incomes ≤ $150 or households ≥ 5 members trigger automated priority alerts.
